  • Multi-language — English and Marathi interface.

Challenges of Present Public Health Complaint System

💾
Duplication of Complaints — same issue filed multiple times from different channels with no deduplication
🔗
Partial Integration with Redressal Mechanism — complaints lodged via 1916 or social media not tracked end-to-end
📋
Complex Registration Forms — citizens unable to specify disease category or severity; poor data quality
📊
Missing Unified Dashboard — no real-time visibility into ward-level complaint load or SLA breaches for management
🗺️
No Location Mapping / Geotagging — field officers cannot locate the exact site; delayed or incorrect dispatch
⚠️
No Early Warning for Outbreaks — fever clusters, dengue and malaria hotspots not visible until reported manually

Proposed Solution

For Citizens

  • Web & Mobile-first complaint registration with GPS auto-detection
  • 4-step guided wizard — category, location, description, review
  • Real-time duplicate warning: surfaces nearby complaints within 100 m
  • Photo evidence upload with automatic compression
  • Unique Complaint ID with shareable tracking link
  • Anonymous filing option — no personal data required
  • Gamification — points, badges and leaderboard to reward civic participation
  • Re-register if resolution is unsatisfactory

For BMC Officers & Field Staff

  • Location-enabled system — exact GPS pin for speedy dispatch and redressal
  • System-enabled duplication handling — auto-grouping of nearby same-type complaints
  • Auto-allocation of complaint to ward-level officer based on GPS location
  • Level 0 (field staff) captures geo-tagged "after" photo to confirm resolution
  • Officer dashboard to view, track and manage assigned complaints
  • Real-time SLA timer — colour-coded urgency with auto-escalation on breach
  • Real-time GIS dashboard for higher officials — heatmap, ward risk, escalated cases
  • AI Chatbot assistant for citizens and officer guidance

Redressal Mechanism — Escalation Levels

Level Designated Officers SLA (Critical) SLA (Severe) SLA (Moderate) SLA (Minor)
Level 0 Sanitary Inspector / Ground Field Staff 4 hours 12 hours 24 hours 48 hours
Level 1 Ward Medical Officer (WMO) / Health Supervisor Auto-escalation on SLA breach from L0
Level 2 District Malaria Officer (DMO) / Asst. Commissioner Auto-escalation + inter-ward transfer approval
Level 3 Chief Medical Officer (CMO) / HOD Escalated if L1 & L2 fail within their SLA
Level 4 Additional Municipal Commissioner (Health) High visibility; requires executive action
Level 5 Municipal Commissioner Critical unresolved cases — top escalation
On successful registration, complaint is auto-assigned to Level 1 (Ward Medical Officer) based on GPS location.
Level 1 officer can transfer complaint to a peer officer (maximum twice); 3rd transfer requires Level 2 approval.
Level 1 assigns to ground staff (L0) for on-site attendance; L0 captures geo-tagged resolution photo.
Unresolved complaints auto-escalate in real time with colour-coded urgency (yellow → orange → red pulsing).
